## GROWTH PHILOSOPHY

> **Product-Led Growth with Cultural Soul**

Every growth mechanism must:
1. **Teach something real** — no empty engagement
2. **Respect the culture** — no gamification of sacred concepts
3. **Compound the moat** — strengthen Knowledge Graph, Cultural Intelligence, or AI Tutor
4. **Serve the segments** — Diaspora, Tourists, Expats each get tailored value

## 3. ENGAGEMENT & RETENTION STRATEGY

### 3.1 North Star Metric

**Weekly Meaningful Darija Learning Sessions (WMDLS)**
> A session where user completes ≥1 of:
> - Translation with cultural context viewed
> - AI conversation ≥5 turns
> - Guide study ≥3 phrases
> - Pronunciation practice ≥5 words
> - Spaced repetition review completed

**Target:** 5,000 WMDLS by Month 12

### 3.2 Retention Curves by Segment

| Segment | D1 Target | D7 Target | D30 Target | Key Driver |
|---------|-----------|-----------|------------|------------|
| **Diaspora** | 60% | 40% | 25% | Kids challenge + family sharing |
| **Tourist** | 45% | 25% | 15% | Trip proximity + offline access |
| **Expat** | 55% | 35% | 20% | Daily life utility + mastery |
| **Blended** | 50% | 30% | 18% | — |

### 3.3 Retention Mechanics (Moat-Aligned)

| Mechanic | Layer | Implementation | Segment |
|----------|-------|----------------|---------|
| **Cultural Streak** | 2 | "7 days of Moroccan hospitality phrases" — not generic streak | Diaspora, Tourist |
| **Mastery Milestones** | 1, 3 | "You mastered 50 marketplace phrases — unlock Souk Guide audio" | All |
| **Pronunciation Challenges** | 4 | Weekly: "Perfect the '3' sound" → badge + feedback | Pro+ |
| **Conversation Depth** | 3 | "Had a 10-turn conversation about family" → cultural badge | Expat, Diaspora |
| **Offline Sync** | 1, 4 | Download guides + audio for trip | Tourist |
| **Family Progress Share** | 2, 3 | Weekly WhatsApp summary to grandparents | Diaspora |

### 3.4 Churn Prevention

| Signal | Intervention | Channel |
|--------|--------------|---------|
| **No session 3 days** | Personalized phrase: "Missed you! Here's a phrase for your situation" | Email + WhatsApp |
| **Stuck on concept** | AI tutor proactive: "Let's practice that grammar point" | In-app notification |
| **Trial ending (Day 5)** | Value summary + cultural milestone + upgrade offer | Email + in-app |
| **Pro inactive 14 days** | "Your mastery dashboard shows X — want a quick session?" | Email |
| **Failed payment** | Grace period + cultural note ("We'd love to keep you") | Email + SMS |

## 4. MONETIZATION STRATEGY

### 4.1 Freemium Model (Validated)

| Tier | Monthly | Annual | Features | Target User |
|------|---------|--------|----------|-------------|
| **Free** | $0 | — | Dictionary search, 5 translations/day, 1 guide, Kids Day 1 | Top of funnel |
| **Pro** | $11 | $99 ($8.25/mo) | Unlimited translator, AI chat, all guides, full Kids, progress dashboard | Core learners |
| **Pro+** | $19 | $179 ($14.90/mo) | + Pronunciation Lab, Mastery Dashboard, Advanced Analytics, Offline Sync, Priority Support | Power users |

### 4.2 Pricing Psychology

- **$11/mo Pro** — positioned between Duolingo ($7) and Speak ($15); signals quality
- **$99 annual** — 25% discount; "2 months free" framing
- **$19 Pro+** — premium tier for pronunciation + mastery; high margin
- **No lifetime deal** — preserves LTV; annual is the commitment

### 4.3 Conversion Funnel Optimization

| Funnel Stage | Current | Target | Optimization |
|--------------|---------|--------|--------------|
| **Visitor → Signup** | ~2% | 4% | Persona landing pages, immediate value |
| **Signup → Trial Start** | ~40% | 55% | Soft triggers, persona-specific value |
| **Trial → Paid (Pro)** | ~15% | 25% | Milestone emails, cultural progress summary |
| **Pro → Pro+ Upgrade** | ~5% | 15% | Pronunciation demo, mastery gaps highlighted |
